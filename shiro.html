Shiro wat recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups shiro flour
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp berbere (optional)
  • 3 cups water
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. Cook the onions until soft.
  2. Add the niter kibbeh, garlic, and berbere. Cook for 2–3 minutes
  3. Stir in the shiro flour.
  4. Gradually add the water while stirring to prevent lumps.
  5. Simmer for 10–15 minutes until thick and smooth.
  6. Add salt to taste and serve hot with injera.

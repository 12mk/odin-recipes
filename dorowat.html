Recipe to make Dorowat


Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Oil
  • Berbere(grounded chili)
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Chicken
  • Habesha spices

Directions

  1. Prepare the chicken.
    • Wash the chicken pieces with water and lemon juice if desired.
    • Pat dry and season lightly with salt.
  2. Cook the onions.
    • Place the chopped onions in a large pot over medium-low heat without oil.
    • Stir often for 20–30 minutes, until they become very soft and lightly browned.
  3. Add the butter.
    • Stir in the niter kibbeh (or butter).
    • Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
  4. Add the spices.
    • Mix in the garlic and ginger and cook for 1–2 minutes.
    • Add the berbere spice and tomato paste (if using).
    • Stir continuously for another 3–5 minutes so the spices become fragrant.
  5. Cook the chicken.
    • Add the chicken pieces and coat them well with the onion-spice mixture.
    • Cook for about 10 minutes, turning occasionally.
  6. Simmer.
    • Pour in the chicken stock or water.
    • Cover and simmer on low heat for 35–45 minutes, or until the chicken is tender.
  7. Add the eggs.
    • Make small slits in the hard-boiled eggs with a knife or fork so they absorb the sauce.
    • Add them to the stew and simmer for another 10–15 minutes.
  8. Taste and serve.
    • Adjust the salt if needed.
    • Serve the Doro Wat hot with injera.
